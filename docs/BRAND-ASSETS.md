# Mahalo Care brand assets

## Authoritative sources

- Brand guide: https://mahalo.health/brand
- Logos: the Mahalo Health brand ZIP supplied by the user.
- Fonts: the font resources referenced by the official brand page's stylesheet.

The supplied **Mahalo Care** product lockup is the appropriate identity for this remote/chronic-care application. It is not a newly constructed wordmark.

## Usage

- Preserve the supplied SVG artwork, color, proportions and composition.
- Use the color lockup on light, quiet backgrounds; the white lockup on sufficiently dark backgrounds.
- The lockup's digital minimum width is 140 px. Use the standalone mark at 24 px or larger when the full lockup cannot fit.
- Leave at least half the symbol's width clear around full lockups and one-third around standalone marks.
- Do not redraw, retype, rotate, recolor, distort, add effects to or rearrange the artwork.
- Canonical mark colors: orange `#FF7A21`, coral `#F44762`, cyan `#20B5D0`.
- Supporting identity colors: charcoal `#1C1D1F`, warm white `#FCFCFA`.
- Display typography: **Victor Serif Smooth**, supplied as OpenType at 400, 600 and 700.
- Body/UI typography: **Noto Sans Mahalo**, supplied as a variable Latin WOFF2.
- Fonts and logos are served from the application's local brand assets rather than depending on third-party font services at runtime.
- Logo assets retain the embedded PNG artwork in the approved SVGs; they are not reconstructed.

Mahalo Health retains ownership of the brand assets. The implementation does not expand the permissions stated in its brand guide. Do not publish font binaries as a separately licensed font product or imply unrelated endorsements.

The brand refresh changes presentation only. Clinical severity colors must remain legible and distinguishable; patient permissions, signatures, billing rules and synthetic-data notices must retain their existing behavior.
